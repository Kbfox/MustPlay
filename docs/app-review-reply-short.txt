Thank you for the review. A screen recording from a physical iPhone on the latest iOS is attached. Answers to each item:

1. RECORDING: Starts on the Home Screen with the app launch, then: list > search (IGDB) > add a game > details > Mark as Completed > 5-star rating + one-line note > completion celebration > share card > tap the locked "Poster" style to open the paywall. The paywall shows title, length and price of every product (Pro Yearly $9.99/year with 7-day free trial, Pro Monthly $1.99/month, Pro Lifetime $19.99 one-time), Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links (Terms is opened) and Restore Purchases. Ends in Settings showing Restore Purchases, Go Pro and the IGDB attribution. No accounts exist, so there is no registration, login or account deletion. No user-generated content is shared between users; notes and ratings are private and stored only on the device.

2. PURPOSE / AUDIENCE: MustPlay is a personal "games bucket list". Players hear about games faster than they can play them and the list ends up scattered across notes and screenshots. MustPlay lets a player save a game the moment they hear about it, keep it in one place (Want to play / Playing / Completed), pick what to play next, mark it completed with a rating and a one-line memory, and share a completion card. Audience: teen and adult gamers on any platform (rated 12+). Single-user utility; no social features, chat or community.

3. SETUP / ACCESS: No login, credentials or sample files. Launch > tap "+" > type any game title (e.g. "Hades") > tap a result to add. Tap a row for details; "Mark as Completed" opens the rating sheet. With 2+ released games in Want to play, a "What next?" card appears at the top of the list. Settings (gear) has Restore Purchases, Go Pro, Terms/Privacy and the IGDB link. Free tier holds 10 games; adding an 11th opens the paywall.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES: IGDB (Twitch) for game metadata and cover art, accessed read-only through our own Cloudflare Worker proxy (no Twitch credentials in the app). RevenueCat for In-App Purchase management and paywall rendering (purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit). OneSignal for optional reminder push notifications (permission requested only after the first game is added). Apple frameworks: SwiftUI, SwiftData (local storage), StoreKit, UserNotifications. No authentication provider, analytics SDK, AI service or ads.

5. REGIONS: No regional differences. Identical features and content everywhere; prices are localized by the App Store. UI is English only.

6. REGULATED / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL: Not a regulated industry. Game titles, dates and cover images come from IGDB under its API Terms of Use, which permit use in commercial apps with attribution (credited in Settings > About). Images load on demand from IGDB's CDN; we do not host or redistribute them. No other third-party material.

7. IN-APP PURCHASES: All three unlock the same "Pro" entitlement: MustPlay Pro Yearly (auto-renewable, $9.99/year, 7-day free trial), MustPlay Pro Monthly (auto-renewable, $1.99/month), MustPlay Pro Lifetime (non-consumable, $19.99). Pro removes the 10-game limit and unlocks the Poster, Retro and Minimal share-card styles. To reach the purchase flow: (a) add an 11th game, or (b) any game > Mark as Completed > on the share card tap a locked style, or (c) Settings > Go Pro. The paywall lists all products with price and duration, Terms/Privacy links and Restore Purchases. Subscriptions are managed in App Store subscription settings.
