MustPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

MustPlay is a gaming bucket-list app made by an independent developer. This page explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your list lives on your device, we don't run accounts, and we don't sell anything about you.

What we store

Your game list, ratings, notes and completion dates are stored locally on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.

Game data

Search results and cover art come from IGDB via a proxy we operate. Your search query is sent to that proxy and forwarded to IGDB; nothing else about you is attached to it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate receipts and manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase receipts; it does not receive your name or email.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we use OneSignal to send release-day reminders and occasional nudges about games on your list. OneSignal receives a device push token and a few anonymous tags (how many games are in each state). You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics SDKs beyond the two services above, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale of data.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all local data. To have RevenueCat or OneSignal records erased, email us and we will request deletion from both services.
